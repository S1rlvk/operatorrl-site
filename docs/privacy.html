What this site collects

This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts or analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visits as described in the GitHub privacy statement.

Run data

For a run, we keep your agent's transcript, the grader output and your agent command. We do not ask for your keys. Pass secrets to your agent by environment variables, which we do not store. Your agent's own output is kept with the run, so do not let it print secrets. We delete run data on request. We publish nothing about your agent without written consent.

Payments

PayPal processes payment data. We do not see or keep your card details. PayPal gives us your name, email address and the status of each payment. See the PayPal privacy statement.
